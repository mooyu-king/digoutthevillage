Privacy Policy

This policy covers how the Dig Out the Village Guide handles information when you browse the site or use its interactive features.

Traffic measurement

The site uses Plausible analytics through data.1back.link to measure visits and use of the installation controls. Requests may include technical information such as page addresses, referral information and browser details. The site does not send checklist selections or email content to its analytics service.

Local storage and app installation

The checklist stores your selections in your browser when storage is available. The installed guide uses session storage for its session indicator. A service worker can store guide pages, images and interface files for later reading. You can remove that data in your browser’s site settings.

Embedded game, video and email

Selecting Start Game connects your browser to the external game host. Choosing to load a video connects your browser to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. Opening a video on YouTube is subject to its privacy practices. If you contact us by email, we receive the address and information you choose to include so we can respond.

Privacy questions

Contact mooyuking@gmail.com about this policy or a message you have sent.
