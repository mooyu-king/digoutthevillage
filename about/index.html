About Us

Dig Out the Village Guide is an independent reference for readers learning how a snowbound village comes back to life. It brings the clearing loop, equipment upgrades and home restoration into one English guide.

What you can use here

Use the illustrated field notes to understand a snowbank before choosing where to dig. The starter route includes a checklist you can mark on your device. Two third-party videos are available from the watch section.

About the imagery

The winter landscapes and equipment illustrations are made for this guide. They visualize the guide’s topics. Select Start Game in the player to load the game from its host.
