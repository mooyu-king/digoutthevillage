The village

A way through the snow

Dig Out the Village is a cozy incremental game set in Valcandil, a village in Lapland buried by a blizzard. You start with a shovel and make room for streets, doors and neighbors to reappear. This Dig Out the Village guide covers the clearing loop, snow layers and workshop upgrades. Select Start Game to play the free demo online in your browser, or watch the clips below.

What makes Dig Out the Village satisfying is the scale of each small change. The first cut reveals how deep the storm drift is. A few passes later, two patches of exposed ground can meet. Clearing the snow around a home lets its residents return, and warm windows tell you that a piece of the village is alive again. The quiet sky and occasional shooting star give you space to appreciate the work as it happens.

First session

A starter route for Dig Out the Village

The opening of Dig Out the Village invites you to clear the square. Choose one side of it first. A route from the open area to a nearby house gives you a small, visible goal and an easy return journey to the workshop. Once that track is obvious, widen the places where you keep turning or crossing. Save larger banks for when you have a tool that makes them worth the effort.

Dig Out the Village also rewards occasional detours. If you uncover treasure, check the immediate area and then use the path you have already established to regain your bearings. The best route is easy to retrace. Looking back at a cleared passage can help you notice how it connects to streets you have yet to uncover.

Progression

Three decisions that shape a clearing route

  1. Give each pass a destination

    In Dig Out the Village, start with the spot that will make the next pass easier. For example, clear a pinch point between two useful stretches. Once they connect, you can approach the bank from either side and see its depth more easily. A clear connection also makes unfamiliar snow less disorienting.

  2. Let the snow choose the tool

    Dig Out the Village asks you to manage the difference between a broad drift and a hard obstruction. If the starter tool is making steady progress, keep clearing. If the bank has turned to ice, head back to the workshop and see whether your current resources can buy a useful upgrade.

  3. Look for signs of a returning village

    Pause after clearing a house in Dig Out the Village and look around its approach. The lit windows and returning residents are part of the reward for digging. From there, identify the nearest house you can reach along your existing tracks. Keep the parts you have uncovered connected as more of the village appears.

Questions

Dig Out the Village FAQ

Can I play Dig Out the Village here?

Yes. Select Start Game to play the free Dig Out the Village demo online on a computer with a keyboard and mouse. The browser demo covers the plaza and the hollow. The videos, illustrated tips and first-session checklist are below the player.

What is the main goal in Dig Out the Village?

Clear snow from the buried streets and houses of Valcandil. As you work through Dig Out the Village, cleared homes let neighbors return. Upgrades help you move beyond the snow and ice that slowed down your first passes.

Does every layer clear at the same rate?

Dig Out the Village distinguishes loose powder, packed snow and solid ice. Ice calls for better equipment. Keep an accessible route between your work area and the workshop so returning with a better tool is straightforward.

Do the clips and illustrations show the same thing?

The clips are the two supplied Dig Out the Village videos. The illustrations explain ideas such as snow layers, a branching workshop upgrade path and a restored doorway. Use their captions to match each scene to the corresponding part of the guide.

Is the checklist tied to game progress?

The Dig Out the Village checklist is a planning aid saved on this device when available. Game saves and progression belong to the game itself. You can change any selection here before or after watching the videos.
