# Dig Out the Village

Play the free browser demo of **Dig Out the Village**, a cozy first-person game about bringing a snowbound village back to life. Dig paths through snow and ice, upgrade your tools, and watch warm lights return to buried homes.

[Play the free demo](https://digoutthevillage.xyz/#game) · [Watch gameplay](https://digoutthevillage.xyz/#watch) · [Read the field guide](https://digoutthevillage.xyz/#mechanics)

![A blue winter village under soft northern lights](dig-out-the-village-village.webp)

The demo covers the village plaza and the hollow and requires a keyboard and mouse. The site also includes an illustrated guide to snow layers and tool upgrades, a starter checklist, two gameplay videos, and [answers to common questions](https://digoutthevillage.xyz/#faq).
